Create your first accessible video

You need a video you can use, space for a local project, and a Gemini API key if you want automated analysis. Choose a short local video for your first walkthrough.

  1. Connect analysis. Open VidiStudio > Settings > Gemini API. Enter your key, choose Load Available Models, and select models for the analysis tasks. Video details and timeline pictures do not require Gemini, but a Gemini connection is required to validate a YouTube video before its project is created.
  2. Create the project. In All Projects, choose New Project. For a local file, select Video File, choose a video, then choose Create Project. For YouTube, select YouTube Video, paste the URL, choose Fetch Details, review the title and picture, and choose Create Project. The YouTube video's visibility must be Public; Private, Unlisted, and every other YouTube visibility setting cannot be used for Gemini video understanding. VidiStudio confirms Gemini access before it creates the project folder. You can instead share one movie from Finder or a public YouTube video page from Safari directly to VidiStudio; the Share extension applies the same validation before creating the project and opening its editor.
  3. Choose complete or individual processing. Use Project > Analysis > Analyze Video to generate the transcript, summary, and Audio Descriptions in sequence. To work one step at a time, use Generate Transcript, then Generate Summary, then Generate Audio Descriptions. When a result exists, the same command becomes Regenerate. To refresh the video information and timeline pictures without AI, use Regenerate Video Details & Timeline Pictures. The inspector shows only the action related to its current editor section. If only certain parts need additional or improved descriptions, use Add Segment > Re-Analyze This Segment for those time ranges instead of regenerating every Audio Description.
  4. Review the script. Play the video and inspect each Audio Description. Correct misleading or unnecessary information. Add missing descriptions and adjust start/end times. Choose Save Segment when editing a segment.
  5. Choose a voice. Open Settings > Narration. Choose Apple on-device voices or Gemini TTS. Select and preview a voice.
  6. Synthesize narration. Return to AD Overview and choose Generate All AD Audio, or use Synthesize Narration in the inspector. If speech does not fit, shorten the text or adjust the available gap, then synthesize it again. Regenerating Audio Descriptions deletes narration associated with the earlier descriptions.
  7. Listen in context. Choose Play synthesized narration in Settings > Narration. Turn on Narration in the editor and play the video. Check that descriptions are understandable and do not obscure essential dialogue.
  8. Export. Open the Export dialog, select Video on the left, then choose one Audio option and one Transcript and Captions option on the right. Choose Save. If a warning appears, follow its plain-language next step or cancel and repair missing narration first.

Before sharing

Check the first and last description, pronunciation of names, on-screen text, transitions between dialogue and narration, and the final audio level. Live Apple Speech preview does not create files for export; generate narration before exporting.
